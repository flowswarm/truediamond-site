Frequently Asked Questions

At True Diamond Life Care Group, we understand that guardianship can raise important questions for individuals, families, and professionals. We believe informed decisions begin with clear communication, transparency, and trust. Below are answers to common questions about our guardianship services.

A court-appointed guardian is a person or qualified entity authorized by the Pennsylvania Court of Common Pleas, typically through its Orphans' Court Division, to make certain decisions on behalf of an individual whom the court has determined to be incapacitated and in need of guardianship services.

Under Pennsylvania law, guardians may be appointed to oversee personal matters, financial matters, or both, depending on the court's findings and order. A guardian has a legal and fiduciary responsibility to protect the individual's rights and best interests while respecting their expressed wishes and preferences to the greatest extent possible.

At True Diamond Life Care Group, we recognize that guardianship is a position of profound trust, requiring sound judgment, ethical decision-making, compassion, and accountability.

Under Pennsylvania law, an adult may need a guardian when a court determines, based on clear and convincing evidence, that a condition or disability impairs their ability to make or communicate decisions and that guardianship services are necessary to meet their essential needs or protect their financial resources.

The court must consider the individual's functional abilities, available support systems, and less restrictive alternatives before appointing a guardian. A diagnosis, disability, or residence in a care facility does not automatically establish the need for guardianship.

True Diamond Life Care Group is committed to recognizing everyone's unique circumstances and fully respecting their right to independence and self-determination permitted by law.

True Diamond Life Care Group focuses on professional, person-centered guardianship and advocacy. Depending on the appointment, authorization, and availability of services, our intended service areas include:

  • Guardianship of the Person: Supporting personal care, living arrangements, healthcare coordination, and other matters authorized by the court.
  • Guardianship of the Estate: Managing financial affairs, safeguarding assets, monitoring income and expenditures, and maintaining appropriate records when appointed and authorized.
  • Person-Centered Advocacy: Promoting individual rights, preferences, dignity, and participation in decisions.
  • Care Coordination: Communicating with healthcare providers, residential facilities, social workers, and other authorized professionals to help address an individual's needs.
  • Court Reporting and Compliance: Maintaining appropriate records and completing required guardianship reports and filings.
  • Professional Collaboration: Coordinating with attorneys, court personnel, families, and other relevant parties when appropriate and legally authorized.

Specific services depend on the authority granted by the court, the needs of the individual, and our capacity to accept the appointment. Please contact us to discuss a potential referral.

A guardianship consultation with True Diamond Life Care Group provides an opportunity to discuss an individual's circumstances, identify potential guardianship needs, and explore how professional guardianship services may be appropriate.

During the consultation, we discuss the individual's current situation, support needs, existing legal arrangements, and any concerns regarding personal well-being, healthcare coordination, or financial oversight. We also review the general guardianship process in Pennsylvania, explain the differences between guardianship of the person and guardianship of the estate, and discuss the responsibilities associated with a potential court appointment.

Our consultation may include:

  • Individual Needs Assessment: Discussing the individual's circumstances, functional needs, available support systems, and areas where assistance may be needed.
  • Guardianship Options: Exploring whether guardianship may be appropriate and discussing less restrictive alternatives that may be worth considering.
  • Legal Process Overview: Explaining the general Pennsylvania guardianship appointment process and identifying questions that may require guidance from a qualified attorney.
  • Scope of Services: Reviewing the types of guardianship services we offer and whether the potential appointment aligns with our capabilities.
  • Documentation and Next Steps: Identifying potentially relevant records and outlining possible next steps, referrals, or information needed to move forward.
  • Fees and Appointment Expectations: Discussing consultation fees, service availability, potential responsibilities, and the court appointment process.

At True Diamond Life Care Group, we approach every consultation with empathy, professionalism, confidentiality, and respect for the individual's dignity. Our goal is to provide clarity, help families and professionals better understand their options, and determine whether our guardianship services may be an appropriate fit.

Please note: A consultation does not establish a guardianship relationship, guarantee acceptance of an appointment, or replace legal advice from an attorney. In Pennsylvania, guardianship authority must be granted by the court.

Professional collaboration is an important part of responsible guardianship. A guardian's responsibilities may include coordinating supportive services, advocating for the individual's best interests, monitoring care arrangements, maintaining appropriate documentation, and submitting required reports to the appointing court.

We recognize the importance of professional accountability, timely communication, confidentiality, and respect for the roles of other professionals involved in supporting the individual.
