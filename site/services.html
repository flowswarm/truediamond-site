Our Services

Guardianship Services in Pennsylvania

At True Diamond Life Care Group, we provide individualized guardianship services designed to protect the rights, dignity, and well-being of individuals who require assistance with personal decision-making. Every service is delivered with professionalism, empathy, integrity, and respect for everyone's unique circumstances.

What we do

Our Guardianship Services

01

Court-Appointed Guardianship

Professional guardianship services for individuals appointed by the court, with responsibilities guided by the applicable court order and governing law.

Learn more
02

Person-Centered Advocacy

Individualized advocacy that recognizes each person's preferences, values, needs, and personal history.

Learn more
03

Healthcare and Medical Decision Support

When authorized by the applicable guardianship order, assisting with healthcare decisions and advocating for appropriate treatment and continuity of care.

Estate and Financial Guardianship

When specifically appointed and authorized, overseeing financial matters, safeguarding assets, and fulfilling required financial reporting obligations.

Maintaining appropriate case documentation, preparing required reports, and meeting applicable court deadlines and reporting obligations.

Professional Collaboration and Case Communication

Working collaboratively with attorneys, court personnel, families, care teams, and other authorized stakeholders.

Who We Serve

True Diamond Life Care Group aims to support individuals across the lifespan who require court-appointed guardianship, subject to applicable law, court authorization, and our service capacity.

  • Adults who have been determined by a court to need a guardian.
  • Older adults who require assistance and protection through a court-appointed guardianship.
  • Individuals with disabilities or other circumstances affecting their ability to make certain decisions, as determined through the applicable legal process.
  • Courts, attorneys, families, and professionals seeking a qualified, compassionate guardian.

Specific services depend on the authority granted by the court, the needs of the individual, and our capacity to accept the appointment.
