Who We Serve

True Diamond Life Care Group aims to support individuals across the lifespan who require court-appointed guardianship, subject to applicable law, court authorization, and our service capacity.

  • Adults who have been determined by a court to need a guardian.
  • Older adults who require assistance and protection through a court-appointed guardianship.
  • Individuals with disabilities or other circumstances affecting their ability to make certain decisions, as determined through the applicable legal process.
  • Courts, attorneys, families, and professionals seeking a qualified, compassionate guardian.

Our Approach

At True Diamond Life Care Group, we believe that effective guardianship should make a meaningful difference in the lives of the individuals entrusted to our care. Our goal is to promote dignity, safeguard individual rights, support personal well-being, and ensure that every guardianship responsibility is approached with compassion, integrity, and accountability.

Through thoughtful advocacy, responsible decision-making, and individualized attention, we strive to achieve outcomes that reflect each person's unique needs, preferences, circumstances, and best interests.

Our Promise

We approach every guardianship with the understanding that we have been entrusted with profound responsibility. While individual circumstances and outcomes may vary, our commitment to ethical service, empathetic advocacy, and professional accountability remains constant.

Specific services depend on the authority granted by the court, the needs of the individual, and our capacity to accept the appointment. Please contact us to discuss a potential referral.
